Fifth session, family (a): 0x71004f09dc, 0x71004f17f8, 0x71004f2620, 0x71004f3448 (1,532 bytes each, one template:
identical opcodes, 33 differing words each against 4f09dc). Drafted as one template, docs/s5_wip/4f09dc.cpp; each of
the four instantiations is 21 instructions off abstracted (1,536 bytes here, 74 instruction diffs, 178 word diffs).

What it is: a contact search in the physics module of the vector five (docs/s5_wip/4eab78.cpp: same epsilon
0.0011920929 at 0x73bbc4/0x73bcbc, the same normalise x * inv, inv * y, inv * z, the same to_vec4). The owner is
{ shape (0x20/0x30/0x30/0x40 bytes), entries[64] of 0x90, u64 count, pad, matrix* }, so the count is at
0x2420/0x2430/0x2430/0x2440 and the shape-specific callee (4f42c0/4f481c/4f475c/4f48dc) takes the owner pointer as
the shape. bool find(Result* out): for each entry, dir = the normal if a and b are equal within the epsilon, else
normalised a - b; skip if dot(normal, dir) > 0; two contact ids (4e7928 fills the second, the shape callee the
first), each trimmed to 22 bits when its kind byte is 2; skip duplicates (an address comparison, see the draft);
append; insertion sort by |a - b|^2; the last hit's points (w = 1) transformed by the matrix, its direction through
the 3x3 part and normalised, the two ids, found = 1.

Settled (each measured):
- The hit's direction type has a default constructor that zeroes w (the 64 stores at +0xc, and the store at the top
  of each iteration); that makes the hit non-POD, so its data size is 0x28 and the sort's assignments copy 0x28 bytes
  (memcpy calls) while the stride stays 0x30.
- The sort is an inlined member function of the hit list: the inliner moves its key alloca to the top of the
  caller's entry block, and stack colouring merges the direction into the key's slot, which gives the original's
  frame (key/direction at x29-0xb0, hits at sp+0x30, the ids at sp+0x20). Written in the caller the key goes below.
- The break form of the insertion loop (int j = i; while (j > 0) { if (!(less)) break; ... }) gives the original's
  counters (x24 = j + 1, the j - 1 index in w25); the && form and the j = i - 1 form are 34-60 off.
- The near test must be written in the caller with a.x read into a local first: in a helper (by value, or by
  reference: static helpers get their reference parameters promoted to values by ArgumentPromotion) InstCombine
  swaps the compare operands, and without the local the a.x load comes last and takes the pre-indexed base.
- The vector five's to_vec4 (12-byte copy + int w) gives the three int loads of the normal with y and z paired.
- The 3x3 result computed into locals and stored after; the final normalise multiplies x and y before storing them.
- The points copied through a local (12-byte copy, w = 1) then assigned; XMVector4Transform from
  misc/matrix_inverse.h (matrix by value).

Still off (21):
- Register allocation in the sort: the original spills the hit count n (stp x19, x9, [sp, #8]) and keeps this in
  x20; ours keeps n in x20 and spills this before the sort, which also reorders the tail (the first transform's row
  loads and the 3x3 products, about 8 of the 21). The pointer form (Hit* p = items + j; *p = *(p - 1); && test)
  keeps this in x20 and fixes the tail transform (105 word diffs) but changes the loop counters (27 off). The
  original's break exit stores through the loop's pointer IV (x22) while the other two exits recompute the address
  from j; no source form tried reproduces that split.
- The direction's w lane: the original's merged w phi is the first phi of the dot block (registers s0, then
  z, y, x in s1-s3); ours is last (InstCombine merges the w stores before GVN creates the x, y, z phis). Moving that
  phi first by hand in the IR gives 20.
- Scheduling of the sort's two squared lengths (the original loads the previous entry pointer in the middle of the
  key's length); irsearch on that block finds 16 but with orders that do not map to source.
Callees: 0x71004e7928 (968 B), the shape callees (4f42c0 20 B, 4f48dc 16 B, 4f475c/4f481c 192 B), sqrtf, memcpy.
